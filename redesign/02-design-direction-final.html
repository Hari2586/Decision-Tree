Solution pages: one system, three structures

Every solution page carries the same blocks in the same order: question, headline figure, calculator, evidence, closing steps, fund categories, questions, documents, next step, sources. The structure that holds them changes with the problem, so two pages never feel like the same page twice.

Timeline · Child Education

A goal far in the future. Navy gradient headline, calculator pinned on the right, evidence as a vertical timeline with orange waypoints and alternating chart tints.

body.page-solution.variant-timeline

Stress test · Emergency Fund

Urgency, read at a glance. Deep-navy band with the figure at 112px beside its sentence, calculator full width with results beside the inputs, evidence as a 2x2 grid of cards, questions in two columns, a navy Next Step block.

body.page-solution.variant-stress

Two paths · EMI Management

A comparison. Honey and wash split headline, calculator pinned on the left, evidence as a swipeable row of cards with snap points, closing steps as numbered cards alternating honey and band, the two product cards marked orange and navy.

body.page-solution.variant-paths

Rule. A variant is a body class and CSS only. Markup, text and section order are identical across the three, so the content lock, the calculators and the verification script are untouched. New solution pages pick the variant whose problem shape matches: far-off goals take the timeline, urgent gaps take the stress test, either-or decisions take two paths.

Motion and interaction

Everything is progressive: the page is complete with no script, and all motion switches off under prefers-reduced-motion.

  • Reading progress bar, 3px orange, along the top edge.
  • Scroll reveal on cards, steps, FAQs and documents, staggered 60ms per item, from a visible resting state.
  • Interactive charts. Hover, tap or focus a bar for a navy tooltip with its label and value; the bar turns orange and the rest fade. The calculator chart gets a hit zone per year and legend toggles.
  • Sliders with a navy filled track and a thumb that grows on hover. Segmented pickers slide their navy highlight.
  • Figure tick. Any number that recalculates flashes orange ink for 550ms.
  • Floating result bar on phones that hides itself while the calculator is on screen.
  • Hover lifts of 2 to 3px on cards; chevrons slide 3px; FAQ folds rotate their chevron and fade their answer in.

Compliance footer

On every page, at body size, verbatim.

Money Honey Financial Services Private Limited · AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor & SIF Distributor · ARN-000000 · Registered 1 Jan 2015, valid till 31 Dec 2027

Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully.
Investments in Specialized Investment Fund involves relatively higher risk including potential loss of capital, liquidity risk and market volatility. Please read all investment strategy related documents carefully before making the investment decision.

Rule. Warnings sit in band-tinted boxes with an orange left edge, never grey small print. The demo notice stays at the top of every page inside the header landmark.

What we avoid

  • Pill buttons, glows and gradients on controls. Controls are flat with 4px corners.
  • Trading-app energy: green and red tickers, charts in the hero, countdown urgency. Gain and loss colours appear on figures only.
  • Purple or blue gradients, floating 3D coins, stock photos of happy families.
  • Emoji as icons. Every mark is a CSS-drawn line icon in brand colours.
  • Orange text below 19px bold. Orange on more than one action per screen region.
  • Any change to copy. Icons, chevrons and tooltips add no visible words.

Developer handoff

Every page links the same stylesheet and script. Generated pages keep their own CSS, which resolves through the compatibility block at the end of mh.css.
